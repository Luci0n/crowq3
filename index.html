▪MAKE IT YOURS

All optional.

YOUR NAME, AND COLORING IT

Set it in Setup → Player, or from the console at any time, even mid match:

/name Crow

Quake 3 colors text with a caret followed by a digit. Everything after it takes that color until the next code:

/name ^1Crow
/name ^1C^7row
/name ^4x^5X^4x
Crow

Type directly in the colored name above. Select visible letters and click a color to recolor them; the Quake ^N command underneath is generated automatically. With no selection, a color applies from the caret to the next existing color change.

CODECOLORLOOKS LIKE
^0Black
^1Red
^2Green
^3Yellow
^4Blue
^5Cyan
^6Magenta
^7White

That is the whole palette. There is no ^8 or ^9, and if you type one it shows up as literal text on the scoreboard.

If the caret will not type, which is normal on Spanish and other Latin American layouts, it is a dead key waiting for a vowel. Press it and then press space and you get a bare ^. Two presses in a row also works on some layouts.

Color codes count toward the length limit even though they are invisible, so a heavily colored name has to be short.

PICKING A CHARACTER

Setup → Player has a portrait you can click through. From the console it is two commands, because the head is separate from the body:

/model sarge
/headmodel sarge

The 23 characters that come with the game:

anarki biker bitterman bones crash doom grunt hunter keel klesk lucy major mynx orbb ranger razor sarge slash sorlag tankjr uriel visor xaero

This server also carries a team color skin pack, so every one of those comes in seven colors. Add the color after a slash:

/model bones/black
/headmodel bones/black

Colors: aqua black green orange pink white yellow. You do not need to download anything, it arrives when you connect.

If somebody shows up as a plain default character, their game does not have that skin yet. It sorts itself out on their next connect.

SETTINGS WORTH CHANGING

None of this is required. Type them into the console, or put them in your config so they stick.

COMMANDWHAT IT DOES
/cg_fov 110Field of view. The default 90 is narrow and makes people circling you vanish off the edge of the screen. Most players use 100 to 120.
/com_maxfps 125Frame cap. 125 is traditional because of how Quake 3 handles jump physics at certain frame rates.
/cg_drawfps 1Frame rate in the corner.
/cg_lagometer 1A small graph showing whether stutter is your connection or your computer.
/sensitivity 5Mouse speed. Lower than you expect if you have come from a modern shooter.
/rate 25000
/snaps 40
/cl_maxpackets 100		 Network settings. The defaults are tuned for 1999 modems and hold you back on anything since.
/cg_drawgun 0Hide the weapon model for a clearer view.
/bind F12 screenshotJPEGA screenshot key. They land in the game folder under screenshots.
